# Implementation exercise in "Parser as Character"

## Exercise

Take a small scene from a parser game, yours or someone else's, and play through the first scene with the transcript on. Then go back and replace every default parser response with a character's voice. This includes errors, refusals and examinations. You can replace the response in the parser itself or just in the transcript. 

## Answer

I chose my game *Hand Me Down*. It's the intro to the parser phase. I'll redo the parser responses as if it were the father looking over her shoulder.
