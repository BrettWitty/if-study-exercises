# Writing exercise in "Parser as Character"

## Exercise

Write ten to fifteen parser responses to unexpected commands in a character voice you define in advance — but don’t write the character description down for the reader. Then hand the responses to someone and see if they can describe the narrator. If they can't, then perhaps the voice is decorative rather than constructive. 

## Answer

(This is an imaginary transcript from a game. Can you describe the PC, the parser-narrator and scene from the responses?)

`> X ME`

Well now, you might not have the most whizz-bang modern components, but by gum, you're solid. I chose 'em myself. Long nights fishing through the scrap bin... or at least the bits I could reach!

`> LOOK`

**Old Pete's Garage**

I'll tell ya that you're in the garage of Old Pete. As a visitor, mind. A perfectly legal visitor, so says the sign. Keep your head down and screwed on, don't make a mess and we'll make this a quick visit.

V's car is just danglin' there up on a hoist, unfinished labour and all. Apart from the jumble of boxes and crap, Old Pete at least keeps a tidy workbench. The shutter door is closed - of course - and the office is locked and dark - of course, of course.

`> X PETE`

Now unless you can see Old Pete hidin' off-camera, the old coot ain't there. That's the point.

`> X SIGN`

Nothing more legally bindin' than an official sign. I can't see it on the feed, but it says: "No persons allowed outside of working hours." I know it says that because I painted it for him. It was his weird ol' way of getting the message through to me by makin' me write it. Well shit... read and acknowledged, Pete. Might I suggest as your own readin' material: *Humanoid ROBOT Act of 2035* wherein you'll find the loophole you, my fellow humanoid, stand distinctly within.

`> INVENTORY`

For legal reasons, you ain't got a bag, a rucksack, a purse or even a dang bindle. You've got your own two mechatronic hands and whatever I bolted into them. Which is currently a LED flashlight and a multi-head screwdriver set.

`> X LED`

Whoa, buddy. Don't go flashing that around. You'll dazzle my feed and excite anyone outside into thinking they might be wantin' to check out the light party inside. And we don't want either of those. We especially don't want both.

`> X HAND`

Your right hand has a LED flashlight screwed into the palm. Just straighten your arm and point it to light up the area. Your left hand has a set of screwdriver attachments you can poke to flip out. Your index finger has an empty slot to put them in.

`> STRAIGHTEN ARM`

Now maybe you didn't get a full firmware update, but I have no doggone idea what you're saying.

`> TURN LIGHT ON`

Oh hey, now. Warn me when you're gonna do that. Switching off the infra-red. Now be careful where you're pointing that thing.

Old Pete's garage is now dimly lit... like its owner.

`> X CAR`

Whoo-wee. She's a beauty. Shame to see her all dangling up there, wheels akimbo, having her delicates manhandled by Old Pete. You'll show her the proper respect, wontcha?

Underneath there should be a tracking module. Coloured green. Don't take the red one, that's V's anti-theft device and you don't want that hullabaloo!
